## What this repository does with the data

- The raw CSV stays in `data/`, which is git-ignored and never committed.
- Personal-information columns (customer name, email, password, street, zip code, city and state) are
  dropped before anything is stored in the database (see section 3 of `docs/design.md`).
- `tests/fixtures/synthetic_dataco.csv` is **not** DataCo data. It is generated by
  `scripts/make_fixture.py` with invented values; only the column names match the real file.
- Reports in `reports/` contain only aggregate counts and rates computed from the data.
